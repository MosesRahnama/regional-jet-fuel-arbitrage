3. Book builder

Select spreads, forecast model, cost and period. Each spread is a price difference between two markets or two delivery months, in US cents per gallon (c/gal).

weekly profit = position × (st+2 − st+1) − cost × |position change|
s = spread price, c/gal · t = week the forecast is made · st+1, st+2 = mean spread price in the next two weeks · position = +1 buy the spread (buy first leg, sell second), −1 sell the spread

West Coast three = LA_GC_1 + LA_JET_M1M2 + LA_JET_M2M3, named before testing. Notebook 01 shortlist = LA_GC_1 + LA_JET_M2M3 + SING_GC_1 + HO_BRENT, chosen from price statistics.

Total · c/galsum of weekly profit

Dollars a year
Position = sign(4-week forecast), set on the weekend after week t.
Sharpe = mean weekly profit / sd of weekly profit × √52. Dollars a year = c/gal a year × 0.42 × barrels.
Try this: West Coast three at cost 0.50: Sharpe 0.72; at 0.75: break-even. Switch the model to Own 26-week mean gap to compare the fair value with the spread's own history.
